## About Me

I am a Full Stack AI and Cloud Engineer who turns ambitious ideas into dependable software. I work across AI engineering, product development, data, and cloud infrastructure, helping teams move from early concepts to systems trusted in production. I focus each decision on user needs and outcomes.

AI products need more than a model connected to an interface. They need sound architecture, structured data, clear workflows, secure services, and infrastructure that grows with demand. I build these foundations with AI capabilities, keeping products useful, observable, maintainable, and resilient.

### What I Do

- 🤖 **AI Engineering:** I build LLM applications, RAG systems, AI agents, multi-step workflows, and practical automation.
- 🧩 **Full-Stack Product Engineering:** I deliver complete applications across React and modern web interfaces, backend services, APIs, databases, and integrations designed around real-world users.
- ☁️ **Cloud, Data, and Operations:** I create scalable cloud environments, data pipelines, deployment workflows, and reliable foundations for AI-enabled products.

I bring a product-minded, systems-first perspective to complex technical challenges. Whether the work is a focused AI capability, a new internal platform, or a complete customer-facing product, I prioritize quality, clear communication, and long-term reliability. The result is software that is not only impressive in a demo, but genuinely valuable in day-to-day operations.
